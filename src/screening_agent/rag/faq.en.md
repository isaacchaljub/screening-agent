# Grupo Sazón — Delivery Driver FAQ (English)

## How much do you pay per delivery?
A base rate per delivery plus a distance bonus for longer routes. Exact rates depend on your zone
and are confirmed by a recruiter — this FAQ won't quote a number that varies by city.

## When do I get paid?
Weekly, by bank transfer, every Friday for the previous Monday–Sunday period.

## Are there bonuses on top of the base rate?
Yes — a peak-hours bonus during lunch and dinner rushes, and a referral bonus if you bring in
another driver who completes their first month.

## What kind of contract is this?
You work as a self-employed driver (autónomo in Spain, an independent contractor in Mexico), not
as a payroll employee. You invoice Grupo Sazón for completed deliveries.

## Are there minimum guaranteed hours?
No fixed minimum — you work the slots you book. Most active drivers average 20–35 hours a week.

## How do shifts work?
You book delivery slots in advance through the driver app, a day or a few days ahead, based on
demand in your zone.

## Can I choose my own hours?
Mostly yes. Availability is flexible, but we ask everyone to cover at least one weekend shift a
month, since that's when demand peaks.

## What vehicle do I need?
A moto, bicycle, or car — whichever suits your zone and local traffic rules. E-bikes are welcome
in most cities.

## Do I need to provide my own vehicle?
Yes, delivery drivers use their own vehicle. It must meet the basic requirements for your zone
(valid registration and, for motorised vehicles, a matching licence).

## What documents do I need to apply?
A valid ID (or NIE/residency document in Spain, RFC in Mexico), your driver's licence if using a
motorised vehicle, vehicle registration and insurance, and bank details for payouts.

## Is there a background check?
We verify your ID and licence are valid and current. There's no criminal background check for
this role.

## Which cities do you operate in?
45 locations across Spain and Mexico, including Madrid, Barcelona, Sevilla, Ciudad de México, and
Guadalajara. A recruiter will confirm whether your city is covered.

## Can I work in more than one city?
You pick one primary zone when you start. Moving zones later is possible — ask your recruiter.

## Am I insured while working?
Yes — Grupo Sazón provides delivery liability insurance that covers you while you're on an active
delivery (from pickup to drop-off).

## What happens if I have an accident during a delivery?
Report it immediately through the driver app's incident button. The insurance covers active
deliveries per the policy terms; a recruiter will walk you through the process.

## Do I keep 100% of my tips?
Yes, tips are entirely yours — they're never shared with Grupo Sazón or deducted from anything.

## What equipment do you provide?
An insulated delivery bag with the Grupo Sazón branding. Everything else (vehicle, phone) is
yours.

## Do I need my own phone and data plan?
Yes, a smartphone with an active data plan to run the driver app is required.

## Is there training before I start?
A short online onboarding covering the app, safety, and delivery etiquette, plus an in-app
walkthrough before your first shift.

## How long does onboarding take?
Usually the same day to 48 hours after your documents are approved, then you can book your first
shift.
